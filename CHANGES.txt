Mood, immediately:
  - The moment you send a message the header reacts (dashed chip, "Annoyed...", a floating "harsh words / kind words" note with the change). No waiting for the model:
    the server reads the tone against the character's personality and triggers. A few seconds later the tracker's real answer replaces it, with a quiet update if it agrees.
  - The header, avatar ring and every reply take the mood's colour; the ring pulses faster when the character is energetic. Each reply keeps a mood tag (😊 Happy ▲4).
  - Reacting to a message (❤️ 👍 😂 😮 😢 😡) is noticed instantly and nudges how they feel (❤️ 👍 +1, 😡 -1). Undo/delete reverses it.
  - Click the chip: mood card, energy, a mood-history chart (click a bar for that moment), and quick controls to set the mood by hand.

Relationships, as progress:
  - Pick a path per character: Friendship, Romance, Partners, Master & disciple (choose who is the master), Rivalry, Family, or leave it Undecided.
    The character behaves accordingly and does not turn romantic on a non-romantic path unless you change it.
  - Six stages per path (e.g. Acquaintances > Friendly > Friends > Close friends > Best friends > Inseparable), shown as a track with a filled bar and a ring in the header.
  - It moves with how they feel about you: kind exchanges build it, hurtful ones damage it faster, and it never falls below 60% of its peak from words alone.
  - Every stage reached pops a milestone card and is kept in the history. Undecided bonds ask "Where is this heading?" once they are close enough.
  - Changing path keeps what you built (friends who become lovers start with a head start). Groups get a list of everyone's mood and bond.
  - Memory tab > Relationships: path, role and progress for every character.
Characters whose sheet says they are under 18 only ever get the friendship-style paths; romance is not offered for them.
